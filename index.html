I work on evaluating language models. At BAIR I am building a benchmark for whether frontier models can repair their own mid-procedure reasoning errors, swept over how much context sits between the error and the correction. Before that I ran a study of whether instructing reasoning models to think in a different language buys accuracy for fewer tokens. It does not.

My interests are long-context evaluation, reinforcement learning, and biological ML. I am also a forward deployed engineering intern at MoolAI, where I harden agents for production.

Research

A self-correction benchmark swept over context length

In progress, Jiao Group, Berkeley AI Research, 2026

Models build a long procedural structure step by step, an error is injected, and the model is asked to find and fix it after a controlled number of further steps. Every metric is a correction metric. The manipulated variable is the distance between the error and the correction.

Budgeted cross-lingual reasoning

Independent study, 2026. Blog post

Four open-weight reasoning models, five instructed reasoning languages, BIG-Bench Extra Hard, per-model token budgets. Language compliance falls from 81 percent on BBH to 36 percent on BBEH, and no reasoning-language switch buys tokens at equal accuracy.
